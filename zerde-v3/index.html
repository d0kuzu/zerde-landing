A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Glad you asked.

AI can feel like a big step.
Let’s make it a little clearer.

Is Zerde a good fit for my business?

Zerde is built for small and medium businesses in the US — from local service teams and wellness studios to tour operators and independent professionals. If you want help with customer inquiries, video content, or AI search visibility, we can explore the right fit in your audit.

Do I need all three solutions?

No. Each solution stands on its own. Start with the area that matters most to your business and add more when it makes sense.

Will the AI assistant sound like my business?

We build its responses around your services, business information, and preferred tone. Booking rules and situations that need your team are agreed during setup.

Can it work with my existing tools?

The booking assistant can connect to channels such as web chat and SMS, and calendars including Google Calendar and Cal.com. We’ll review your CRM and other tools before confirming the setup.

Can you guarantee AI will recommend my business?

No one can guarantee placement in AI-generated answers. We focus on making your business easier to understand and discover through clear information, useful content, and a consistent digital presence.

What happens after the free audit?

We’ll walk you through the opportunities we find and discuss a focused next step. You can decide whether a Zerde solution is right for you — there’s no obligation to move forward.
